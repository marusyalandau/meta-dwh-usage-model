Q3: What does a team spend its resources on?
Split by query category (read / write / maintenance / metadata) and client.
Sums and shares, not averages: one large MERGE and many small SELECTs
would give a meaningless average per query.

query_category | source   | queries | cpu_hours | cpu_share_pct
---------------+----------+---------+-----------+--------------
read           | datagrip | 713     | 4.64      | 38.4         
write          | airflow  | 367     | 3.41      | 28.2         
read           | jupyter  | 526     | 3.17      | 26.2         
maintenance    | airflow  | 36      | 0.59      | 4.9          
read           | airflow  | 40      | 0.25      | 2.1          
metadata       | datagrip | 469     | 0.01      | 0.1          
metadata       | jupyter  | 76      | 0.0       | 0.0          
(7 rows)

Metadata noise: many cheap queries (e.g. DataGrip introspection)
inflate query counts but cost almost nothing.

query_category | queries | query_share_pct | cpu_share_pct
---------------+---------+-----------------+--------------
read           | 4150    | 66.9            | 86.77        
metadata       | 1647    | 26.6            | 0.15         
write          | 367     | 5.9             | 11.15        
maintenance    | 36      | 0.6             | 1.93         
(4 rows)

Distinct users per category over the period.
Counted from the query-level fact: distinct counts cannot be summed
from the daily aggregate (active_user_count is per row only).

query_category | distinct_users
---------------+---------------
read           | 8             
metadata       | 6             
maintenance    | 1             
write          | 1             
(4 rows)

Who writes each table: a hint for the table owner (dim_table.owner_team_id).
Only 'write' queries: maintenance (optimize) also rewrites tables,
but it is run by the platform, not by the table owner.

iceberg.analytics.daily_active_users   | data_engineering | svc_airflow | 119          
iceberg.analytics.sessions_daily       | data_engineering | svc_airflow | 129          
iceberg.marketing.campaign_performance | data_engineering | svc_airflow | 119          
(3 rows)
